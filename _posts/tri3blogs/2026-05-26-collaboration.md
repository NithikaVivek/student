# Page 5: Collaboration & Technical Cohesion

## Learning That Software Engineering Is a Team Sport

Throughout the onboarding project, I worked closely with classmates including Xavier, Yash, and Risha to make sure different levels connected smoothly.

This experience changed how I viewed technical collaboration.

At first, I assumed teamwork in coding mostly meant splitting tasks.

Instead, I learned that technical collaboration requires:

* Constant communication
* Shared standards
* Flexibility
* System integration

Even if one level worked perfectly on its own, the entire experience could fail if systems did not connect smoothly.

This pushed me to think more holistically about development. I had to consider how my work interacted with everyone else’s rather than focusing only on my own section.

I also became better at explaining technical ideas clearly—an underrated but essential engineering skill.
